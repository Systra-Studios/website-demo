A spare part in five days instead of five months

A transport operator needed a plastic part that is no longer made. Here is how we solved it.

A transport operator faced a typical problem: a small plastic part in the interior had broken, and the manufacturer no longer supplied it.

We measured the part, created a drawing and 3D printed it from an approved material. Five days later it was installed.
