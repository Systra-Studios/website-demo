When 3D printing pays off

3D printing shines when quantities are small or shapes are complex. You do not need a mould as with injection moulding.

  • Spare parts that are no longer available
  • Prototypes and pre-series
  • Small series up to about 1,000 units

Materials

We print in PA12, PA11 and stainless steel, among others. We work out the right material with you based on the part’s requirements.
